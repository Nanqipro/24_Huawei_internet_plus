# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve this competition archive.

Please open an issue before a large change and include:

- the problem or use case;
- a minimal video-free reproduction when possible;
- Python, OpenCV, ONNX Runtime, operating-system, and execution-provider
  versions;
- whether the issue affects local inference, event aggregation, or ModelArts.

Do not upload private driving videos, credentials, competition-only datasets,
or other material you do not have permission to redistribute. New behavior
logic should include a focused unit test.
